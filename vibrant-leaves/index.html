vibrant leaves

Synthetic images for training image restoration networks, generated in your browser.

VibrantLeaves generates synthetic images on which image restoration networks (denoising, super-resolution, …) can be trained instead of natural images. Random shapes occlude each other (the dead leaves model), filled with pseudo-periodic and 1/f textures, in three depth-of-field layers. Choose the parameters, and drop a few photos to sample the colours from. See the paper and the code, which also generates full datasets. Images are generated in your browser; your photos are not uploaded.
